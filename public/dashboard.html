Pipeline

PHASE 1 · BEFORE THE PODCAST — finding and approving what to record
1
Find topic ideas SYSTEM

The AI reads every past topic (950+ across the whole show history) and recommends 5 new ones that fill real gaps — each with a category, priority, and a duplicate check so nothing already covered gets suggested again.

2
Add the good ones to the Gap Tracker YOU · 1 CLICK

"Add to Gap Tracker" writes the full row (title, category, priority, dedup note, status "Open Gap") straight into the 🔍 Gap Tracker tab of the TD Podcast Topic Tracker sheet.

3
Pitch to Gary & Naren IN THE TRACKER

From the Gap Tracker, topics are pitched via the 💡 Suggestions tab. Accepted ones move to the 📅 Recording Pipeline tab. This step happens inside the Google Sheet, not in this system.

4
Record the episode GARY + NAREN

The recording itself, plus transcript cleanup. When the clean transcript is ready, Phase 2 starts below.

PHASE 2 · AFTER THE PODCAST — the automated production line (you touch it 3 times)
1
Paste the clean transcript YOU · TOUCH 1

Create the episode on the Pipeline page and paste the transcript. Everything below starts automatically.

2
AI writes the show notes SYSTEM

SEO description, timestamps, takeaways, title tag, and both CTA links — written from the transcript in about a minute.

3
Approve the show notes YOU · TOUCH 2 — GATE 1

Read, then Approve — or type what to change and the AI rewrites it. On approve, the webmaster ticket is built ready to copy-send, and the newsletters start writing themselves.

4
AI writes both newsletters SYSTEM

Weekly + weekend emails as ready-to-send HTML, previewed exactly as subscribers will see them.

5
Approve the newsletters YOU · TOUCH 3 — GATE 2

Check the previews on the Approvals page and approve. That's your last touch before release.

6
Drafts land in Kit SYSTEM

Both emails are created as draft broadcasts in the Kit account. Audience rules (TD tags, cold subscribers excluded) are confirmed in Kit before scheduling, per the Kit SOP.

7
Release day runs itself SYSTEM

On the release date the scheduler verifies the episode is live and pulls the KPIs — downloads, clicks, reach — compared against the last 4 episodes.

8
Leads are scored & drafted SYSTEM

Everyone who clicked the newsletter is pulled and scored: HOT clicked the strategy-meeting link, WARM read the episode. A personal message is drafted for each.

9
Send the follow-ups personally YOU — THE PAYOFF

Copy each draft and send it from your own account, then mark it. Pricing/timing questions go to BDR the same day. This is how episodes become booked strategy meetings.

Everything the system does is logged in Activity, and results land in Analytics plus the Topic Tracker sheet the whole team can open.
